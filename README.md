## The word

**maybe**

## Why delete it?

`maybe` is the universal hedge word — the one word that lets everyone dodge
committing to anything. Delete it and the whole world has to answer yes or no.
No more "maybe I'll come", no more "maybe later", no more "maybe" as a
complete answer to a direct question. Every flake, every non-answer, every
polite dodge loses its favourite shelter in a single stroke. It is the
smallest word with the biggest blast radius: five letters holding up an entire
economy of indecision.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the word `maybe` to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else.
